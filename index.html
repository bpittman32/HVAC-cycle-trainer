Training model, not a design or certification tool. Properties come from a Peng-Robinson equation of state fitted to published pressure-temperature charts (typically within 1–3 psi). Blends with glide use bubble point for subcooling and dew point for superheat. Heat exchangers use a moving-boundary effectiveness model; charge is tracked by refrigerant mass in every coil and line.
